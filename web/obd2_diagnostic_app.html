Diagnostic snapshot

              
Readiness, vehicle information & freeze frame

            

            
          

          

            Reads supported standard services: Mode 01 readiness, Mode 09
            vehicle information and Mode 02 freeze-frame 00. Availability varies
            by ECU. This read does not erase data. VIN and calibration
            identifiers may appear in the snapshot and copied report.

On-board monitor tests · Mode 06

              
Read-only test records reported by the ECU

            

            
          

          

            Test IDs and raw counts need the correct protocol, scaling and
            vehicle-specific service information. A raw value inside a numeric
            limit does not establish a pass or diagnose a component. This reader
            does not infer rich-to-lean behavior or replace manufacturer test
            procedures.
